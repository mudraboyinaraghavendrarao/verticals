About Us

Established in 2008 DTEL is a Global IT, Construction and Engineering Services firm based out of Richardson, Texas. With a broad portfolio of Services and solutions, DTEL helps organizations evolve their IT ,Civil, Electrical and Engineering needs constantly and seamlessly, from ideation and development to execution.

In the age of constant technological innovation, DTEL has a Trustworthy leadership with combined experience of more than 100 years. Our vast technology and industry expertise enable us to partner with clients to deliver sophisticated solutions rapidly and on budget. We have positioned ourselves to be a “Single Point of Contact” and “One Stop Shop” for all your needs in Digital Transformation, Telecom ,Retail and Renewable Energy space.
